Where we are

The apartments are minutes from Patuxent River Naval Air Station and the Patuxent River Naval Air Museum, with everyday shopping and dining close by. The surrounding area rewards people who like being outdoors and near the water: Point Lookout State Park, where the Potomac meets the Chesapeake Bay, is a favorite for fishing and history, and Historic St. Mary’s City — Maryland’s first colonial settlement — and the waterfront town of Solomons Island are both an easy drive.

Getting around

The base and daily errands are only a few minutes away, and Washington, D.C., is under an hour and a half up the road.
